# Smothered Smoked Turkey Tails

Smoked turkey tails baked low and slow with vegetables in a rich gravy.

## Ingredients

- 6 smoked turkey tails
- 1/2 green bell pepper, chopped
- 1 stalk celery, chopped
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 Tbsp butter
- 4 Tbsp all-purpose flour
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 2 chicken flavor bouillon cubes
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp parsley flakes
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Steps

1. Rinse the turkey tails with cool water.
2. Chop the peppers, celery, and onion into small to medium size pieces.
3. Combine the broth and flour, then stir well until the flour is dissolved to create a slurry.
4. Preheat oven to 350°F.
5. Place the chopped vegetables into a baking dish.
6. Add the turkey tails, bouillon cubes, and seasoning.
7. Add the butter and slurry.
8. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 3 hours total.
9. After 2 hours, remove the foil and stir the gravy, then recover and continue baking for the final hour.
10. Serve with rice, potatoes, or egg noodles. Rice is best.

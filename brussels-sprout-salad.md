# Brussels Sprout Salad

A crunchy shaved-sprout salad with apple, avocado, bacon, and a simple lemon dressing.

## Ingredients

### Salad

- Brussels sprouts
- 2 apples, preferably green
- 2 avocados, ripe
- Sliced almonds
- 1 package bacon

### Dressing

- Lemon juice
- Olive oil
- Mustard powder

## Steps

1. Cook the bacon until extra crispy; cool and crumble.
2. Stem the sprouts, halve them, and cut into thin slices.
3. Peel (optional) and dice the apples into approx. 1 cm cubes.
4. Pit and dice the avocados into approx. 1 cm cubes.
5. Mix everything together (optional: hold off on the almonds).
6. Make the dressing and toss it into the salad.
7. Top with almonds if desired.

## Step Details

### 1. Cook the bacon

Cook a package of bacon at about 400°F until extra crispy, about 30–40 minutes. The other steps can be completed while the bacon cools. When done, cool and crumble.

### 6. Make the dressing

Mix equal parts lemon juice and olive oil, add some mustard powder, and shake. Toss into the salad.

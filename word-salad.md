# Word Salad

A not-entirely joke version for pasta salad that uses alphabet noodles. Great for a potluck?

## Ingredients

### Salad

- 2 cups (about 12 oz) dry alphabet noodles
- 2 stalks celery, finely diced
- 1/4 red onion, finely diced
- 1/2 red bell pepper, finely diced
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped (optional)

### Dressing

- 3/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more for the pasta water
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Paprika, for dusting (optional)


## Steps

1. Cook the alphabet noodles, drain and rinse.
2. Whisk the dressing ingredients together in a large bowl.
3. Fold in the noodles and vegetables until evenly coated.
4. Chill at least 1 hour, then stir, adjust seasoning, and dust with paprika before serving.

## Notes

- Alphabet noodles are much smaller than macaroni, so they cook fast — start checking at 4 minutes and err on the firm side, or the salad turns to mush.
- The salad thickens as it chills; loosen it with a splash of milk or a spoonful of mayo if needed.
- Spell out a message on top with reserved noodles for maximum potluck impact.

# Gnudi

Ricotta-spinach dumplings with a tomato-butter sauce.

## Ingredients

### Gnudi

- 12 ounces (1½ cups) whole-milk ricotta
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- 1 ounce Parmesan, grated (½ cup), plus extra for garnishing
- 1 tablespoon panko bread crumbs
- ¾ teaspoon table salt, plus salt for cooking gnudi
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- ¼ teaspoon grated lemon zest
- 10 ounces frozen whole-leaf spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
- 2 large egg whites, lightly beaten

### Sauce

- 4 tablespoons butter
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced thin
- 12 ounces cherry or grape tomatoes, halved
- 2 teaspoons sherry vinegar
- ¼ teaspoon table salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper
- 2 tablespoons shredded fresh basil

## Steps

1. Drain the ricotta on paper towels for 10 minutes.
2. Mix the gnudi dough and roll into 45–50 one-inch balls.
3. Start the sauce: brown the garlic in butter, then add tomatoes and vinegar off heat.
4. Simmer the gnudi for 5 minutes.
5. Finish the sauce, plate, and garnish.

## Notes

Ricotta without stabilizers such as locust bean, guar, and xanthan gums drains more readily. You can substitute part-skim ricotta for the whole-milk ricotta. Frozen whole-leaf spinach is easiest to squeeze dry, but frozen chopped spinach will work. Squeezing the spinach should remove ½ to ⅔ cup of liquid; you should have ⅔ cup of finely chopped spinach.

## Step Details

### 1–2. Making the gnudi

Line a rimmed baking sheet with a double layer of paper towels. Spread ricotta in an even layer over the towels; set aside and let sit for 10 minutes. Place flour, Parmesan, panko, salt, pepper, and lemon zest in a large bowl and stir to combine. Process spinach in a food processor until finely chopped, about 30 seconds, scraping down sides of bowl as needed. Transfer spinach to the bowl with the flour mixture. Grasp the paper towels and fold the ricotta in half; peel back the towels. Rotate the sheet 90 degrees and repeat folding and peeling 2 more times to consolidate the ricotta into a smaller mass. Using the paper towels as a sling, transfer the ricotta to the bowl with the spinach mixture. Discard the paper towels but do not wash the sheet. Add egg whites to the bowl and mix gently until well combined.

Transfer heaping teaspoons of dough to the now-empty sheet (you should have 45 to 50 portions). Using your dry hands, gently roll each portion into a 1-inch ball.

### 3. Starting the sauce

Melt butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add garlic and cook, swirling saucepan occasionally, until butter is very foamy and garlic is pale golden brown, 2 to 3 minutes. Off heat, add tomatoes and vinegar; cover and set aside.

### 4. Cooking the gnudi

Bring 1 quart water to a boil in a Dutch oven. Add 1½ teaspoons salt. Using a spider skimmer or slotted spoon, transfer all gnudi to the water. Return water to a gentle simmer. Cook, adjusting heat to maintain a gentle simmer, for 5 minutes, starting the timer once the water has returned to a simmer (to confirm doneness, cut 1 dumpling in half; the center should be firm).

### 5. Finishing and plating

While gnudi simmer, add salt and pepper to the sauce and cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until tomatoes are warmed through and slightly softened, about 2 minutes. Divide sauce evenly among 4 bowls. Using a spider skimmer or slotted spoon, remove gnudi from the pot, drain well, and transfer to the bowls with sauce. Garnish with basil and extra Parmesan. Serve immediately.

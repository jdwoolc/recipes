## Hot Sauce

Honestly, can't go wrong here.

### Ingredients

- Peppers (any amount)

#### Optional

- Garlic
- Carrots
- Fruit (pineapple is nice)

### Steps

1. Combine the peppers (and any optional add-ins) with basic brine.
2. Ferment, then blend into sauce.
